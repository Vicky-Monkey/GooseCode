    Method & interpretation

    Smith–Waterman local alignment finds the highest-scoring region: identical character +2, mismatch −1, each gap −2. One optimal alignment is shown. When scores tie, the first maximum endpoint in reference-then-query order is selected. Sequence ends are not forced into the alignment.

    Identity = identical columns / all alignment columns, including gaps. Coverage = aligned original characters / full sequence length. Ambiguous letters such as X, B, Z and J are literal, not wildcards; * also occupies a position. High local identity may describe only a short region, so check coverage as well. This tool does not calculate statistical significance or establish homology.

    All positions are 1-based and inclusive, relative to the current reference or query, not the original DNA. Local alignment supports up to 200,000 characters per sequence and a length product of up to 80,000,000, inclusive. Progress and cancellation remain available for large comparisons.
